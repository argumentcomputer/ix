# Bounded ix corpus contact

Ingress measured 2026-08-25; C2 compilation boundary updated 2026-09-08.

The first production-writer contact is complete without changing any ingress
or compilation default. It is a byte-exact, one-member fat `.ixc` containing a
small declaration closure over `Std`, generated by ix revision
`6f18ea907b78d06f7dc0917c43beb385561c35f4` (tree
`c09cfe3df1ee6ff92009672cc9077b4b7e2db63a`) with
`leanprover/lean4:v4.33.1`.

The checked-in fixture and full reproduction commands live in
[`fixtures/ixon-std-contact`](../fixtures/ixon-std-contact). It contains the
exact production manifest and `.ixe` bytes as reviewable lowercase hex; no
Compilatrix reencoder occurs in the path.

## Candidate inventory

The available complete Palomar catalog is about 49 GiB across 19 fat members.
Its smallest member is 769,419,219 bytes and contains 268,119 constants, so
even that member exceeds the current per-catalog constant and in-memory work
policies. The available TruthMines material is an incomplete roughly 101 GiB
tree, and the small historical `.ixe` artifacts use ixon v0 rather than the v2
wire. Those measurements are evidence for streaming/mmap ingestion and
chunk-wise certificate scheduling; they are not grounds to silently widen the
bounded defaults.

The selected contact imports real `Std` and asks ix to close one theorem,
`CompilatrixStdContact.addComm`. It is intentionally a bounded semantic
contact, not a claim that the complete standard library now fits the
monolithic pipeline.

## Compilatrix outcome

The exact bytes pass manifest parsing, external ixon-v2 sharing recognition,
all object and semantic address checks, every Merkle commitment, aggregate
budgets, and store-wide member coherence. `Pipeline.checkProgram` then
deterministically rejects constant
`a3b83ad0799bcd3d1c70c01df44141d79e7358e1f23d7d7e5cf9e01dbdc73c85`
with `UsageCheck.UsageErr.freezeNeeded`.

C2 corrected the ownership classification of fresh reusable closures. The
original rejected constant `131533e96baa7f50…` and its unchanged 17-constant
dependency slice now pass usage checking. C3's checked reuse of identical
erased blocks advances the slice through IxIR₀ readdressing to a missing
`Covered` certificate at its root. The [coverage gate](source-coverage.md)
retains its complete addressed artifacts and three accepted upstream exports.

That result is a successful ingress contact and an honest current-fragment
boundary: the fixture is not rewritten to evade the usage policy. `Tests.lean`
pins the raw sizes and hash, manifest provenance, roots, every counter, and the
exact downstream rejection. The filesystem path can be exercised with:

```sh
lake exe catalog-contact -- path/to/std-contact.ixc
```

The harness exits nonzero on either ingress or pipeline rejection, so this
fixture currently prints the roots and counters and then exits 1 at the frozen
`freezeNeeded` result. Scaling contact to the complete available corpora now
requires the separately tracked streaming/chunk-scheduling architecture.
